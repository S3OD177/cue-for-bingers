Bingers, right where you watch

Cue keeps your Bingers library one click away, without opening another tab.

Up next in the toolbar

See the next episode of every show you're watching and tick it off in one click.

Auto-tracking

Turn on the sites you watch on. When you finish an episode or movie, Cue marks it watched. Undo is one tap away.

Your status on IMDb and TMDB

A small badge on title pages shows whether it's in your library, with Follow, Watch later, Mark watched and Rate.

Search from anywhere

Search the Bingers catalog from the popup, or highlight a title on any page and right-click "Search Bingers".

Private by design

Talks only to the Bingers API. No analytics, no ads, no tracking. No site access until you turn a site on.

You stay in control

Pick the threshold (80–95%), the minimum video length, and whether to confirm before anything is marked.

Set up in a minute

You need a Bingers account on the Legend or Icon plan, which includes Bingers API access.

  1. Add Cue to ChromeInstall the extension and pin it to your toolbar.
  2. Connect BingersSign in with Bingers, or paste a personal access token from bingers.app/integrations.
  3. Turn on your sitesEnable the sites you watch on one at a time, or all websites at once. Then just press play.

Questions

Is this made by Bingers?

No. Cue is an independent extension built on the public Bingers API. It isn't made by, endorsed by or affiliated with Bingers, IMDb or TMDB.

Why do I need the Legend or Icon plan?

Bingers only offers API access on those plans. Without it, Bingers answers Cue's requests with "forbidden".

What can Cue see on the sites I watch on?

Only on sites you turn on, and only while a video plays: the video's progress and the title the page shows. It never reads what you type or your browsing elsewhere. The privacy policy has the details.

My site isn't recognised. What now?

Use "Suggest it" or "Report" in the popup, or open an issue with the site's name.
