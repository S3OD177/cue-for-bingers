Welcome to Cue

Three quick steps and your episodes mark themselves watched in Bingers.

  1. 1

    Connect your Bingers account

    Loading…
  2. 2

    Turn on the sites you watch on

    Chrome asks you to approve each site, and Cue only runs on the sites you approve. Pick some now, or later from the Cue icon while you're on any site.

  3. 3

    Play something

    When you finish an episode or movie, Cue marks it watched in Bingers, with Undo. On IMDb and TMDB title pages you'll see your status too.

    Tip: pin Cue to the toolbar. Click the puzzle-piece icon, then the pin next to Cue for Bingers.
