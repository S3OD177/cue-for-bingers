Auto-tracking

Sites

Turn on the sites you watch on. Chrome asks you to approve each one. Cue only runs on sites you approve. For any other site, open it, click the Cue icon, then “Auto-track this site”.

On IMDb and TMDB

When this is on, the extension sends the IMDb or TMDB ID of the title page you're viewing to Bingers to look up your status. Nothing else from the page is read or sent.
