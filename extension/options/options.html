Sites

To turn on a site, open it, click the Cue icon, then “Track”. Chrome asks you to approve each one, and Cue only runs on sites you approve. Your sites are listed below.

On IMDb and TMDB

When this is on, the extension sends the IMDb or TMDB ID of the title page you're viewing to Bingers to look up your status. Nothing else from the page is read or sent.

Something not working?

Copy debug info and send it to the developer with a short description. It includes your settings, the sites you turned on and Cue's last few requests to Bingers (titles and statuses, no sign-in token). Nothing is sent until you paste it somewhere.
